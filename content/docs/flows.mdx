---
title: Flows
description: The six Circuit flows and when each one fits.
---

A flow is a repeatable work pattern. Each flow routes a task, runs the right
blocks in order, carries context forward, and checks its own work along the way.

You rarely pick a flow by hand. `/circuit:run <task>` routes to the right one. If
you want a specific flow, you can name it.

## The six flows

### Build

Adds a new capability. Build grounds itself in the repo, plans the work, and
implements in slices with a verify loop on each slice.

### Fix

Repairs a defect. Fix reproduces the problem, isolates the cause, makes the
change, and proves the fix holds.

### Review

Checks existing work. Review reads a diff or a branch and reports findings ranked
by how much they matter, with evidence.

### Explore

Builds understanding. Explore maps a subsystem or answers a "how does this work"
question without changing code.

### Prototype

Tries an idea quickly. Prototype favors a fast, throwaway path to see whether an
approach is worth pursuing.

### Pursue

Drives an open-ended goal. Pursue holds a durable objective and works toward it
across steps, leaving a short outcome with evidence and residual risks.

## What every flow gives you

- **Routing** picks the next block instead of asking you each time.
- **Evidence** records what ran and what it found.
- **Checkpoints** stop for your judgment only when the result depends on it.
